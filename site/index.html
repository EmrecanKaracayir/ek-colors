Codemos palette v2, next to v1

Eight hues plus brown, solved live in this page. Each colour sits exactly on its contrast target: the harder of WCAG 4.5 and the APCA bar, never below it and only as far above as hex rounding forces. All aim at one CAM16 chroma, measured as each colour appears on its own base, so equal numbers look equally colourful there; a colour can take its own target, and where the screen can't reach one the colour goes as far as it can (▼ in the table). Yellow is the anchor: you set its hue (a little warmer than pure yellow's 111 keeps a darkened yellow from turning olive), and the other hues move within their names until every neighbour is equally different, measured in CAM16-UCS (made for differences this large). The muted version keeps each hue at half its chroma on the same contrast target; brown sits at 60% of the global target. Bases: #EBEBEB light, #181818 dark.

CAM16 reference hues · the yellow ruled lines on the wheels

CAM16 places the hues that look pure, neither reddish nor greenish and so on, at fixed angles; the hues between are described as mixes of their two neighbours ("Y 10 G": yellow with 10% green). Solid lines on the wheels are the unique hues, dashed ones the halfway mixes.
